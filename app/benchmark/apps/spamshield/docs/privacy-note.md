# Privacy note: e-mail filtering (published on the intranet and linked from the e-mail footer policy)

ACME Corp (controller, dpo@acme.example) automatically scans inbound e-mail with a spam filter to
protect its systems, based on its legitimate interest in IT security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Suspicious
messages are held in quarantine for 30 days and can be released by the recipient. Classification
logs contain only a hashed message reference and are kept for 180 days. Senders and recipients have
the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection and may complain to a
supervisory authority.
