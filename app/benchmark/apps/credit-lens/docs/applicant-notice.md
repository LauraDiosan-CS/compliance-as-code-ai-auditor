# How we assess your loan application (information for applicants)

We use automated decision-making based on a statistical credit-scoring model to assess
applications. For loans up to EUR 25,000 the decision can be made automatically; larger amounts are
always reviewed by an underwriter.

**Main factors** that influence the decision: your income and existing obligations
(debt-to-income ratio), your credit bureau score and payment history, length of employment and
number of open credit lines. If your application is declined, the letter lists the main reasons.

**Your rights.** You can contest an automatic decision, express your point of view and obtain a
review by a member of our staff within 5 working days via "Contest decision" in your application
portal. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(c) GDPR. Controller: ACME Consumer Finance S.A.,
dpo@acme-finance.example. Retention: 5 years (legal obligation). You can lodge a complaint with your
supervisory authority.
