# Candidate privacy notice (excerpt shown on the careers portal)

**Controller:** ACME Corp, Rue de la Loi 1, 1000 Brussels. DPO: dpo@acme.example.

**What we use your data for.** To assess your application for the role you applied for. We use an
AI system to rank applications by relevance to the job description; a recruiter reviews every
application and takes all decisions. No decision is made solely by automated means.

**How the ranking works.** The main factors are the match between your skills, experience and
education and the requirements of the role. Your name, photo, age, gender, nationality and contact
details are not used by the model.

**Legal basis.** Steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate
interest in an efficient recruitment process (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Recipients.** ACME recruiters and hiring managers; our hosting provider (AWS, EU region).

**How long we keep your data.** Your CV file: 30 days. Your structured profile: 180 days after the
recruitment round closes, then anonymised. Records of AI rankings and recruiter decisions: 400 days,
without your CV content.

**Your rights.** You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and
data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Contact
dpo@acme.example.
